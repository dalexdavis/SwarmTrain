SwarmTrain Client Policy Planner

This standalone planner helps a client simulate how workload routing changes when they prioritize raw compute, energy efficiency, or carbon footprint. The world map uses OpenStreetMap tiles through Leaflet, and the compute nodes shown here are demo locations.

Policy toggles and trade-offs

A client that only wants speed can set energy efficiency and carbon footprint to zero. A client that cares about sustainability can increase those weights and route work toward cleaner, more efficient regions.
